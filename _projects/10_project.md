## Background

Education systems in low-connectivity settings often rely on fragmented reporting, delayed data collection, and inconsistent school-level records. Better public-sector decision-making depends on reliable pipelines for census, validation, aggregation, and reporting.

## Platform Scope

This work contributed to education data systems for school census and school information management in resource-constrained environments. The emphasis was on data quality, operational reliability, and workflows that could function under field constraints.

## Main Components

- Field data collection for school-level reporting
- Validation and cleaning of large-scale education datasets
- Fault-tolerant ingestion and reporting workflows
- Support for public-sector school profiling and decision-making

## My Role

At **IBM Research Africa**, I contributed to the system design and implementation work that supported the School Census Hub and School Information Hub research outputs.

## Outputs

This work informed publications at **IEEE ICSE-SEIS 2017** and **ACM ICTD 2017**, and reflects an earlier strand of my work on trustworthy data systems in constrained environments.
