## Background

In bandwidth-constrained environments, large content downloads can be slow, fragile, and expensive for individual mobile devices. Low-end devices also operate under strict battery, storage, and reliability constraints that make naive download strategies impractical.

## System Idea

This work developed cooperative downloading logic so nearby or related devices could share parts of the download burden. The goal was to improve completion rates and efficiency without assuming high-end hardware or stable connectivity.

## Design Features

- Segment-based cooperative downloading
- Scheduling based on battery level, storage capacity, and device reliability
- Better use of limited bandwidth in constrained environments
- Practical support for low-end mobile devices

## My Role
