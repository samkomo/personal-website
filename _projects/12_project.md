## Background

Agricultural decision-making often depends on fragmented farm records, variable reporting quality, and limited analytical support for cultivation assessment. Structured modelling of farms, crops, and cultivation activity can improve evidence-based recommendations and operational visibility.

## System Idea

This work contributed to an analytics system that combined structured agricultural data with machine-learning support for cultivation-quality assessment. The approach treated farm and crop information as a connected data problem rather than a collection of isolated records.

## Focus Areas

- Modelling of farm, crop, and cultivation data
- Support for cultivation-quality assessment and evidence-based decisions
- Use of structured relationships to improve downstream analytics
- Practical application in agriculture-focused research and product development
