## Background

Blockchain-based peer-to-peer energy trading systems can suffer from on-chain settlement overhead, limited throughput, and avoidable counterparty friction. If every micro-transaction is committed directly to a ledger, the coordination cost can become too high for practical energy-market usage.

## Framework Idea

EnergiPay models an off-chain payment-channel approach for energy trading. The aim is to preserve verifiable settlement while reducing the number of transactions that must be written to the underlying blockchain.

## Design Focus

- Payment-channel settlement for future energy-credit token models
- Lower on-chain overhead for repeated peer-to-peer exchanges
- Reduced counterparty risk through structured settlement logic
- Better fit for high-frequency or fine-grained energy-trading interactions

## My Role

Within the UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy research context, I contributed to the broader programme around secure distributed energy systems and related coordination models that informed this work.

## Output

EnergiPay was published at **ACM e-Energy 2024** and forms part of the broader research track on scalable, privacy-aware, and interoperable distributed energy markets.
