## Background

Demand-response programs need coordinated visibility into aggregate flexibility, but the underlying bids and operational signals can reveal sensitive information about individual participants. A useful system must balance privacy, verifiability, and coordination efficiency.

## Framework Idea

CypherChain combines **secure multiparty computation** and **homomorphic encryption** to support privacy-preserving aggregation for blockchain-based demand response. The framework enables coordination while reducing disclosure of participant-level information.

## Key Elements

- Private aggregation of demand-response offers
- Confidential computation over sensitive energy data
- Verifiability without broad exposure of underlying participant inputs
- Analysis of computation, communication, and privacy trade-offs

## My Role

As a **PhD Researcher** at UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy, I:

- Designed the privacy-preserving aggregation architecture
- Evaluated the trade-offs of the cryptographic design using smart-building energy data
- Published the resulting work as a research output

## Output

CypherChain was published at **IEEE ICBC 2024** and represents a core privacy contribution within my research on secure distributed energy systems.
