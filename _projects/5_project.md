## Background

Flexibility markets for distributed energy resources depend on trustworthy device behaviour and reliable coordination under adversarial conditions. False data injection, demand manipulation, and weak device trust can distort market signals and reduce confidence in decentralised energy platforms.

## Framework Idea

PlexiChain explored secure flexibility aggregation for distributed energy resources. The work focused on how blockchain-backed coordination can remain useful under hostile or manipulated operating conditions.

## Research Focus

- Secure flexibility aggregation in distributed energy settings
- Resilience against false data injection and coordinated demand manipulation
- Adversarial evaluation of coordination and aggregation logic
- Support for secure market interaction across heterogeneous participants

## My Role

As a **PhD Researcher** at UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy, I developed and evaluated the framework as part of my wider work on secure distributed energy systems and market coordination.

## Output

PlexiChain was released as an **arXiv preprint** and helped shape later thinking around resilient coordination, security layering, and interoperability across energy-market architectures.
