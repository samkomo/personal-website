## Background

Distributed energy ecosystems increasingly rely on multiple ledgers, coordination layers, and domain-specific services. Without interoperability, data and value become trapped inside isolated blockchain deployments, making cross-system coordination difficult.

## Framework Idea

BAILIF addresses interoperability across heterogeneous blockchain networks without requiring shared consensus. The framework uses decentralised notaries and attestation-oriented mechanisms to support secure exchange and coordination.

## Core Contributions

- Blockchain-agnostic interoperability design
- Attestation and notary-based verification for cross-system exchange
- Proof-of-concept implementation spanning multiple blockchain environments
- Practical analysis of how interoperability can support broader distributed energy coordination
