## Background

Immutability is often treated as a defining strength of blockchains, but strict permanence can become problematic in data-sensitive and operationally constrained environments. Prosumer-side energy trading systems may need stronger control over storage growth, governance, and exceptional record handling.

## Framework Idea

Temporary Immutability explored a removable blockchain architecture for prosumer-side energy trading. The objective was to reduce storage overhead while retaining meaningful auditability and governance safeguards.

## Design Themes

- Governed record removal under defined conditions
- Retention of accountability and audit value
- Lower storage pressure for distributed energy deployments
- Practical trade-offs between permanence, efficiency, and operational control

## My Role

As a **PhD Researcher** at UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy, I contributed to the design and analysis of the removable-ledger approach within the wider research track on secure distributed energy systems.

## Output

This work was published in the **Journal of Network and Computer Applications** and broadened the discussion of how blockchain properties should be adapted to practical system needs.
