## Background

Large-scale decentralised energy trading requires coordination across geographically distributed participants, heterogeneous assets, and shifting interaction patterns. Traditional single-chain designs struggle to scale cleanly in this setting.

## Framework Idea

HARB uses a hypergraph-based representation to support scalable coordination in distributed energy systems. The approach captures richer interaction structure than simple pairwise models and helps organise transaction routing and clustering strategies.

## Research Contributions

- Hypergraph-based modelling of decentralised energy interactions
- Adaptive coordination for scalable consortium-style blockchain operation
- Support for clustering, interoperability, and distributed transaction routing
- Foundation for later work on privacy and interoperability in the same research stream

## My Role

As a **PhD Researcher** at UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy, I designed and evaluated HARB as one of the early core frameworks in my doctoral work.

## Output

HARB was published in the **IEEE Internet of Things Journal** and became a foundational part of my research on secure distributed energy systems.
