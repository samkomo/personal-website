## Background

Interoperability in distributed energy trading involves more than just message exchange between systems. It also depends on how prosumers, aggregators, markets, and infrastructure components relate to one another at a structural level.

## Framework Idea

This work proposed a relational network model for analysing interoperability in distributed energy trading. Hypergraph-style representations were used to reason about multi-party interactions that are difficult to capture in simpler network abstractions.

## Key Points

- Structural analysis of interoperability across multiple energy-market actors
- Hypergraph-inspired representation of relationships and dependencies
- Support for reasoning about coordination boundaries and exchange pathways
- Early conceptual groundwork for later interoperability frameworks

## My Role

As a **PhD Researcher** at UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy, I developed the model as a short-form research contribution within the broader energy-systems research programme.

## Output

The work was published at **IEEE ICBC 2020** and helped frame later thinking around interoperability and cross-system coordination.
