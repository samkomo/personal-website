Professional Summary

Applied security and privacy researcher with over ten years of experience across academia, national research, and industry. My work spans GuardlyData, CSIRO Data61, CSIRO Energy, UNSW Sydney, and IBM Research Africa. I design secure and trustworthy systems for sensitive data, critical infrastructure, and multi-party coordination. My experience covers AI security, privacy-preserving computation, applied cryptography, cybersecurity, and secure distributed systems. I have built research systems, prototypes, and enterprise-facing security architectures across generative AI data-loss prevention, cyber-physical energy infrastructure, blockchain-enabled coordination, secure data sharing, and privacy-preserving analytics. My current work focuses on AI security and privacy, including sensitive-data detection, local-first privacy controls, prompt and source-code exposure risks, shadow AI governance, and secure enterprise adoption of generative AI.
