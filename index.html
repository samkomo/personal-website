Professional summary

Samuel M. Karumba is an applied security and privacy researcher with more than a decade of experience across academia, national research and industry. His work spans GuardlyData, CSIRO Data61, CSIRO Energy, the University of New South Wales and IBM Research Africa. He designs secure and trustworthy systems for sensitive data, critical infrastructure and multi‑party coordination. His experience includes AI security, privacy‑preserving computation, applied cryptography and secure distributed systems. He has built research prototypes and enterprise‑facing security architectures for generative‑AI data‑loss prevention, cyber‑physical energy infrastructure, blockchain‑enabled coordination, secure data sharing and privacy‑preserving analytics. Outputs include peer‑reviewed publications, granted patents, technical reports, deployed prototypes and contributions to national research programmes.

Research interests

  • AI security and privacy: sensitive‑data detection, secure enterprise adoption of generative AI and real‑time governance of AI interactions
  • Privacy‑preserving computation: zero‑knowledge proofs, secure multiparty computation and homomorphic encryption
  • Security and privacy of distributed, decentralised and cyber‑physical systems
  • Trust‑aware coordination in distributed systems, including consensus and cross‑system interoperability
  • Applied cryptography for infrastructure, fintech, energy and multi‑party data‑sharing systems
