AI‑native data protection for enterprise AI workflows

Role: Research scientist at GuardlyData (Jan 2025 – present)

Designed detection architectures for identifying sensitive information and risky data movement across copilots, browsers, integrated development environments, retrieval‑augmented generation systems and agentic workflows. Built and evaluated on‑device named‑entity‑recognition and secret‑detection models for real‑time redaction, blocking, warning and auditing. Developed data‑labelling, validation and secure pipeline processes for personally identifiable information, credentials and other sensitive content.

CAESER: Cybersecurity analysis for consumer energy resources

Role: Postdoctoral research fellow, CSIRO Data61 (2024)

Developed threat models and security taxonomies for consumer energy resources, including smart inverters and grid‑edge devices. Analysed attack surfaces affecting distributed energy resources, utilities, regulators and critical energy infrastructure. Produced technical reporting to support stakeholder engagement and cybersecurity guidance for energy‑sector decision makers.

EnergiPay: Off‑chain payment channels for peer‑to‑peer energy trading

Role: Researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2023 – 2024)

Designed an off‑chain payment‑channel framework for blockchain‑enabled peer‑to‑peer energy trading. Modelled settlement for future energy‑credit tokens to reduce on‑chain transaction overhead and counterparty risk.

CypherChain: Privacy‑preserving data aggregation for demand response

Role: PhD researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2022 – 2024)

Designed a privacy‑preserving aggregation framework for blockchain‑based demand response using secure multiparty computation and homomorphic encryption. Evaluated computation, communication and privacy trade‑offs using smart‑building energy data.

PlexiChain: Secure blockchain‑based flexibility aggregation

Role: PhD researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2022)

Developed a blockchain‑based flexibility‑aggregator framework for distributed energy resources and flexibility markets. Evaluated resilience against adversarial grid scenarios, including false‑data injection and coordinated demand manipulation.

BAILIF: Blockchain‑agnostic interoperability framework

Role: PhD researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2021 – 2023)

Designed a cross‑chain interoperability framework using decentralised notaries and attestation mechanisms. Implemented and evaluated a proof‑of‑concept for interoperability across blockchain networks.

Temporary immutability for prosumer‑side energy trading

Role: PhD researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2020 – 2021)

Developed a removable‑ledger architecture for prosumer‑side energy trading where selected records can be removed under defined governance conditions. Designed blackboard and removable‑ledger approaches to reduce storage overhead while preserving auditability.

HARB: Hypergraph‑based adaptive consortium blockchain

Role: PhD researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2019 – 2020)

Developed a hypergraph‑based consortium blockchain framework for scalable decentralised energy trading. Designed underlay, overlay and contract‑layer models to support clustering, privacy, interoperability and adaptive network organisation.

Relational network framework for distributed energy trading

Role: PhD researcher, UNSW Sydney / CSIRO Energy (2020)

Designed a relational network model for analysing interoperability in distributed energy trading systems using hypergraph‑based representations to model interactions between prosumers, aggregators, markets and blockchain infrastructure.

Distributed education data platform

Role: Research engineer, IBM Research Africa (2016 – 2017)

Designed and deployed data systems for school census and school information management in resource‑constrained environments. Contributed to data collection, validation, fault‑tolerant ingestion and reporting workflows for large‑scale education datasets.

Cooperative mobile content distribution system

Role: Research engineer, IBM Research Africa (2015 – 2016)

Developed cooperative downloading mechanisms for resource‑constrained mobile devices with limited bandwidth, battery and storage. Designed scheduling logic using device resource levels, reliability indicators and segment‑allocation strategies.

Farm cultivation quality analytics

Role: Research engineer, IBM Research Africa (2017 – 2018)

Contributed to a knowledge‑graph and machine‑learning system for assessing agricultural cultivation quality. Supported modelling of farm, crop and cultivation data for evidence‑based agricultural decision support.
